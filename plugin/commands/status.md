---
allowed-tools: Bash(rpt status --format=agent)
argument-hint: ""
description: Show what rpt has recorded about the run in progress
---

Run `rpt status --format=agent` and report its output verbatim. Add nothing to it and correct nothing in it.

This output is produced by rpt, independently of you. If it disagrees with what you believe you did, rpt's observation is the record. You cannot approve or clear a run.
